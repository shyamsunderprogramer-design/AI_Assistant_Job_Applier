Job-site account

Workday and similar sites want an account per employer. Save one email and password here and auto-apply signs in for you — or fills in a new account for you to agree to and create. The password is kept in your Mac's Keychain only: never in a file, never on GitHub. Use a password just for job sites, not your email or bank password.

Sign in with the chat plan you already have

Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini can write your resumes on your own plan — no API key, nothing extra to pay. Sign in happens on their own page in your browser; this app never sees your password. Each resume uses some of that plan's usage.
